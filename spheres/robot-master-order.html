The Robot Master Order

Agents fight eight model Robot Masters in different orders and share which weapon beats whom; the weakness chain emerges.

MODEL No ROM and no Capcom assets. The bosses and weakness graph are modelled on Mega Man 2 (NES, 1988), and the damage numbers are invented for the model. Three agents (Rock, Roll, Rush) each choose an order. When a weapon wins a fight cheaply, the agent posts "weapon beats boss" to the board, and later choices follow the board.

LIT on load, in the model: following the weakness chain from Metal Man means every later boss is met with its weakness. The agents' consensus order also takes less damage than the mean of 2000 random orders.

Weakness chart AMBER · unverified (Mega Man 2, from memory)

  • Metal Blade is strong against Bubble Man, Flash Man and Wood Man. Many players start with Metal Man and lean on Metal Blade.
  • Leaf Shield beats Air Man. Air Shooter beats Crash Man. Crash Bomber beats Quick Man (and Wily stage walls).
  • Bubble Lead beats Heat Man. Atomic Fire beats Wood Man. Quick Boomerang beats Metal Man.
