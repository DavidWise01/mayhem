AMBER The asphalt is a joke on the stair. Eight houses, then back to the mouth. The stair is the Petersburg twin, and it climbs instead of looping.

WALL The gold dash is the return, not a second street. No one lives at a number this page did not write.

MODEL A plat. The gray band is the road. The boxes are the doors.
