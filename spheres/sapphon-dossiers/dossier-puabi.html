LIT Bio

Puabi was an important woman of the First Dynasty of Ur in Sumer, around 2600 BCE. Her intact tomb in the Royal Cemetery, excavated by Leonard Woolley in the 1920s, held a cylinder seal with her name and an elaborate gold headdress. Her exact rank is debated, but she clearly held high status.

Source: Wikipedia: Puabi (“Queen of Ur”), checked 2026-10-17 (Wikipedia REST summary: page exists, identity matches).
