Method · How a Sapphon Dossier Is Scored

Every dossier pairs a sourced biography (LIT) with a score card built on the architecture Dave and I built for PATRICIA, the NES agent. The score card is interpretive (MODEL): it describes documented influence and style, never character, and it is not a fact about anyone.

MODEL Each person gets a hand-set base score 0–10 per layer from the documented record (my judgement). The three depths follow PATRICIA's now / this session / long memory:

  • depth 1 · in their moment = base
  • depth 2 · across their life = (2·base + BLUE) / 3
  • depth 3 · long memory = (base + GREEN) / 2
  • layer score = mean of the three depths

MODEL Lanes, streams, tempo, radials, index

  • RED: what happened to them and how they are remembered. BLUE: how they responded. GREEN: was it successful (legacy). Each is hand-set 0–10 with a one-line rationale on every card.
  • Streams as a style profile: ls :: patricia :: learns,reacts = mean(4 logic, −3, −2); toph :: centers,observes = mean(−5, −6, −1); rs :: probes,warily = mean(3 pathos, 2 mythos, −4), shown as shares of their total.
  • Rose triad as tempo: observe .3 = mean(−2, −5) (the two hearing layers); respond .2 = −3 sound{act/react}; sub id .1 = mean(−1, +1); tempo = (.3·observe + .2·respond + .1·sub id) / .6.
  • Radials, 36° per point on a 360° dial: {i} = layer 1 {i}; {id} = mean of the lower layers −1…−6 (drives / reaction, as in v44); {sid} = mean(+1 super id, sub id). As in v44, {sid} carries both sub id and super id because the note is ambiguous.
  • Sapphon index = (mean of the 12 layer scores + mean of the 3 lanes) / 2.

LIT Every formula above runs on Dave's I-13 VM on every page and is compared against JS and the JSON file; any mismatch throws.

Roster rules

LIT 144 people, 12 eras × 12, 6 women and 6 men in every era (72 / 72), from all nine regions used here. Each bio is 2–3 sentences, checked on 2026-10-17 against its Wikipedia page through the REST summary: the page exists, matches the person, and years given in the summary match ours (five dates were corrected this way).

WALL No securely named individuals are attested for 3600–3200 BCE: writing only begins around 3300–3200 BCE (Uruk proto-cuneiform, Abydos tomb U-j labels). The roster starts with Narmer (c. 3100 BCE), among the earliest securely named rulers.

AMBER 15 people are flagged AMBER because their historicity, dates or authorship are debated (for example Gilgamesh, Kubaba, Homer, Laozi, Zoroaster). Early eras lean towards Egypt and Mesopotamia because that is where names survive; regions (actual): Africa 32, Europe 25, Middle East 21, East Asia 21, South Asia 15, Americas 15, Central Asia 6, Oceania 5, Southeast Asia 4.

MODEL The choice of who counts as influential is mine and is not canonical. 4 living people are included; their cards stick to documented public work.
