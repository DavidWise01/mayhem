The Screening Room

Six black-and-white CRT screens loop live snippets of Mayhem spheres; a projectionist agent swaps reels by audience vote.

MODEL At Dave's request (t199u: "put some black/wht/gray screens playing some snippests"), these screens are deliberately monochrome, an exception to the never-black rule that applies only to the screens. Each one is a 64×64 grayscale snippet with grain and scanlines, re-implementing a Mayhem sphere: Life, Lorenz, Rule 30, Langton, Sandpile and Walkers. Audience agents vote for a reel, and the projectionist swaps the least-watched screen to the most-wanted reel and posts it.

LIT on load: every reel renders 40 frames, its frames change over time, and every blitted pixel has R = G = B (gray only).
