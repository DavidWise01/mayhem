The Kakeya Conjecture in 3D

Hong Wang and Joshua Zahl proved that any set containing a unit needle pointing in every direction must be fully three-dimensional (dimension 3), even though its volume can be zero.

LIT Wang and Zahl (arXiv, Feb 2025) proved that every Kakeya set in ℝ³ has Minkowski and Hausdorff dimension 3, settling a roughly 50-year-old problem. Besicovitch (1919/1928) showed such sets can have zero volume. In 2D, Davies (1971) proved dimension 2.
