The Busy Beaver Race

Agent Turing machines with 2 states race to write the most 1s and halt; the board keeps the record.

AMBER Radó (1962) defined the busy beaver. For 2 states and 2 symbols the champion writes 4 ones and halts after 6 steps (standard reference, not re-fetched here).

MODEL The agents are random 2-state machines. When one beats the record it posts its table, and the others mutate from the best table on the board.

LIT on load: all 12^4 = 20736 two-state machines are enumerated with a 100-step cap. The maximum number of 1s among the ones that halt must be 4, or the page throws.
