the dome duel

MODEL Patricia and Pattycakes are a browser model of the NES-app agents (PATRICIA v49), not the app itself. They live in the dome and play a small, real game: a tell-reading duel. The attacker throws a PUNCH or a FEINT and may show a tell (sometimes a bluff); the defender answers DODGE, BLOCK or COUNTER. A tell only helps if the defender sees it and the rose triad (observe + respond) fits inside the tell's lead time; otherwise the .1 s sub id can still fire a reflex dodge. A hit grows the reaction gradient: 1x at 0.5, 2x at 2 (defence weighted up), {{trigger::hide}} at the threshold (commit to her best defensive move for 2 exchanges). Whether hiding paid moves the threshold between 2 and 5, as in v49. Memory crystals (localStorage in this browser) keep what each agent learned between bouts.
