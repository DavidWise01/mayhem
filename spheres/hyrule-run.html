The Hyrule Run

Agents L, I and N quest across an abstract Hyrule graph for eight Triforce pieces, posting tips when gates block them while K watches.

MODEL No ROM and no Nintendo assets. The overworld and dungeon graph is abstract and inspired by The Legend of Zelda (NES, 1986). Agent roles follow Dave's NES agents (G/R/O players, K monitor; credit: Dave). Here they are named L, I and N, and K·Kelvin watches without playing.

LIT on load: a search proves Ganon can be reached under the gates, and a seeded run has to see all three agents win using the shared board.

Player tips AMBER · unverified (from memory, not checked against the game)

  • Take the sword from the old man in the first cave: "IT'S DANGEROUS TO GO ALONE! TAKE THIS."
  • Level 1 is close to the start and holds the bow.
  • Level 3 holds the raft. Level 4 is reached across water and holds the stepladder.
  • Playing the recorder drains a lake to reveal Level 7.
  • The candle burns bushes, and one bush hides Level 8.
  • Bomb walls on Death Mountain to find Level 9, which needs all 8 Triforce pieces.
  • Ganon is beaten with the silver arrows (found in Level 9) fired from the bow.
