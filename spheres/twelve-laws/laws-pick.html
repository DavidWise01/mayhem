Pick's Lattice

A 4 by 2 lattice rectangle has area 8, equal to its interior points plus half its boundary points, minus one.

LIT The rectangle with corners (0,0) and (4,2) has area 8. The page counts lattice points: 3 strictly inside, 12 on the boundary. Pick's formula i + b/2 − 1 returns 8.

AMBER Georg Pick, 1899. The formula is for a simple lattice polygon. This page checks one rectangle by counting, which is the whole identity for this shape, not a sample of a larger claim.

MODEL Gold points are inside. Blue points are on the boundary. The carpet is the rectangle, drawn double-sided so it reads from the balcony camera.
