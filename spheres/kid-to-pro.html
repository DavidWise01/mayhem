Kid → Pro

Learner agents practise a game level by level; their times fall on the power law of practice, and they swap tips that speed each other up.

AMBER The power law of practice, T(N) ≈ a·N^(−b) (Newell & Rosenbloom, 1981) (standard reference, not re-fetched here).

MODEL After Dave's Kid→pro levelling idea (as relayed, MODEL), five learners from Kid to Pro each practise with a hidden learning rate b and noise. Every time a learner beats its personal best it posts a tip, and anyone who reads the tip gets a small one-off boost.

LIT on load: a log–log least-squares fit over seeded practice times (with tips off) recovers each learner's hidden b within 10%.
