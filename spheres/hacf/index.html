How these pages work

MODEL Four arcs follow the show's four seasons: I The Clone (BIOS, clean rooms, luggables, 1981–84), II Mutiny (boards, modems, online games, 1978–90), III The Valley (viruses, IPOs, hypertext, 1986–95) and IV The Web (Gopher, Archie, Mosaic, Netscape, directories vs crawlers, 1990–95). Each page has a 3D pixel scene, a toy to play or poke (a BIOS POST screen, a hex-dump checksum, a modem handshake, a BBS terminal, an ANSI painter, a MUD, tank and breakout minigames, Erlang B modem pools and more), real history facts with sources, live checks, an improvement loop with two agents (ARTIST + CRITIC, or BUILDER + WEAVER + CRITIC), a gallery and JSON/SVG/OBJ exports.

LIT History facts are tagged LIT and carry a source; AMBER marks approximate figures; MODEL marks fiction, the show references and my art; WALL marks what is not done (no network, no real ROM code, no show art).

MODEL Original pixel cast: ROXY ROM (the punk coder), DEX DEALER (the slick salesman), WRENCH (the hardware engineer), MADGE MODEM (the operations lead), BOSS BEIGE (the boss upstairs), KID CRAWLER (the teenage web builder). Sapphon tie-in: every agent is a sapphon coloured by its CRT phosphor (P3 amber, P1 green, P4 white, P7 blue), one per arc.
