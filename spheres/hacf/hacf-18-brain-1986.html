The history

  • LIT 1986 · Brain (January 1986), written by the brothers Basit and Amjad Farooq Alvi in Lahore, Pakistan, is counted as the first IBM PC virus. (Brain (computer virus))
  • LIT 1986 · Brain infects the boot sector of 360 KB floppy disks and spreads when an infected disk boots a machine. (Brain (computer virus))

Improvement loop · ARTIST+CRITIC

MODEL ARTIST changes the look (pixel block size, scanlines, palette snap, CRT curve, exposure, phosphor glow) and CRITIC renders a 128×96 test frame, reads its pixels and scores five things: palette fidelity, contrast, scanlines, exposure and pixel blockiness (weights come from this page's seal). A change is kept only if the score goes up. LIT Scores are measured from pixels, never guessed, and the page throws if the best score ever goes down or doesn't reproduce.
