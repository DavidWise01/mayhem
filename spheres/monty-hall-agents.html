The Monty Hall Agents

Stayer and switcher agents play the three-door game; the switchers' tally climbs toward two-thirds and converts the stayers.

AMBER The Monty Hall problem (Selvin 1975; popularised by vos Savant 1990): if the host always opens a goat door, switching wins with probability 2/3 (standard result, not re-fetched here).

MODEL Agents start out half stayers and half switchers. After each round an agent copies the strategy of a random agent who did better, and the tally board is shared.

LIT on load: exact enumeration of car position, first pick and the host's choice gives P(switch wins) = 2/3, and 30000 seeded games land within 0.01 of it.
