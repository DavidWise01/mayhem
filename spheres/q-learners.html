The Q-Learners

Explorer agents learn a maze by Q-learning and share one Q-table; the greedy path settles on the shortest route.

AMBER Q-learning (Watkins, 1989): Q ← Q + α(r + γ max Q′ − Q) (standard reference, not re-fetched here).

MODEL Four explorers share one Q-table, which is the board. Each step costs −1, the goal pays +10, and ε-greedy exploration decays over time. New best path lengths get posted.

LIT on load: after seeded training, the greedy policy reaches the goal in exactly the shortest-path length found by breadth-first search.
