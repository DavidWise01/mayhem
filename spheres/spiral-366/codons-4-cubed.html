LIT Standard genetic code (NCBI table 1): 4 bases (U, C, A, G) taken 3 at a time give 4³ = 64 codons. 61 are sense codons for 20 amino acids, and 3 are stops: UAA (ochre), UAG (amber), UGA (opal). AUG codes Met and is the start. Leu, Ser and Arg have 6 codons each; Met and Trp have 1 each; Ile has 3.

MODEL On the helix, codon i is rung i, at height round((i + ½)·366/64), in UCAG order. That placement is mine, not biology.

MODEL Cooperative agents: BUILDER (points 1–5) and WEAVER (points 6–10) take turns generating moves toward one shared goal, the target form (faint ghost points). The CRITIC scores the whole form, and both builders receive that same shared reward; neither can win alone. Every page also posts its best shared reward to a cross-page board in localStorage, so agents on all the Spiral 366 pages share one team score.
