    MODEL TORUS STARS · DAVE'S MODEL · ANTIMATTER
    Antimatter stars: by CPT symmetry an antistar would shine exactly like a star. You could only tell them apart by the 511 keV and pion-decay gamma rays where it touches ordinary matter.
  • Gravity Stars
    MODEL TORUS STARS · DAVE'S MODEL · GRAVITY
    Gravity stars: the objects that are mostly gravity. Black holes are real. Gravastars and boson stars are hypothetical. Gravitational-wave sources are where we actually "see" them.
  • Annihilation · E = mc²
    MODEL TORUS STARS · ANNIHILATION
    When matter meets antimatter, 100% of the rest mass becomes energy. One gram of each gives about 43 kilotons of TNT.
  • Positronium & the 511 keV Line
    MODEL TORUS STARS · ANNIHILATION
    An electron and a positron briefly orbit each other as positronium, then annihilate into gamma rays of exactly m_e c² = 511 keV.
  • The Galactic 511 keV Glow
    MODEL TORUS STARS · ANNIHILATION
    The Milky Way's centre glows at 511 keV: positrons annihilate there at about 10⁴³ per second, and their source is still a mystery.
  • Baryon Asymmetry
    MODEL TORUS STARS · ASYMMETRY

  • CP Violation · Kaons

  • CPT Symmetry
    MODEL TORUS STARS · SYMMETRY
    Flip charge, mirror space and reverse time together, and physics stays the same. That is why an antistar would look exactly like a star.
  • ALPHA · Antihydrogen Spectrum
