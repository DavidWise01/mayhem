  • STEWARD II · MAY'S MAP MODEL → The Logistic Fork LIT

  • STEWARD III · BAK'S HEAP MODEL → The Mayhem Pile LIT

    Iterated prisoner's dilemma, payoffs T5 R3 P1 S0, strategies meet in a round robin.
  • STEWARD VII · LAGRANGE'S ARMS MODEL → The Double Swing LIT
    Double pendulum: two rods, one gravity, sensitive chaos; RK4 keeps energy honest.
  • STEWARD VIII · LANGTON'S HIGHWAY MODEL → Langton's Mayhem Ant LIT
    Langton's ant: chaos for ~10,000 steps, then a highway; the rule runs backwards exactly.
